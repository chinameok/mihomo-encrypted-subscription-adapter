# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We expect participants to be respectful, direct, and constructive. Discuss
ideas and evidence rather than people. Welcome good-faith questions, useful
corrections, and different backgrounds.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, doxxing, publishing another person's private information, and
deliberately posting credentials or other sensitive data.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove material that violates this
standard and may restrict participation when necessary. Report safety or
conduct concerns privately through the security contact described in
SECURITY.md; do not include credentials in the report.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant in spirit and is
kept deliberately short for this small project.
